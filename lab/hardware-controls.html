Hardware controls

A stepped strip (a fader lying down) and a mixer, after Braun and Teenage Engineering, next to the control LAAS uses today. Tap a stop or drag along the strip; keyboard: arrows, Home/End. The knob was dropped on 5 Nov: it fits none of the settings rows, so it could not be used everywhere.

Test only. Not in the design system, not in Figma, not on any screen. For testing with 3–5 musicians before anything is adopted. Findings and test plan: notes/research/hardware-controls.md.

1 · Deposit on signing

Settings › Band › Deposit. Today's three presets, as stops.
Now · select
Default on every new offer, full payment inside 28 days of the gig
Strip · 3 stops

Might be betterAll the options and the chosen one are visible without opening anything, and the amount in DKK shows as you move.Might notA select with three options is one tap too, and the strip takes a whole row.

2 · Reminder policy

Settings › Money › Reminders. An ordered choice, gentle to firm.
Now · select
Calm: 7 days before, due date, 3 days after, then by hand. Recommended adds a free reminder at +7. Tight adds the first fee reminder at +10 automatically (still behind Confirm).
Strip · 3 stops, gentle to firm

Nothing is sent without a tick

Might be betterShows where you sit on the scale from gentle to firm, which a closed select hides; the schedule updates as you move.Might notEach option needs its explanation anyway, and the select's help text already gives it.

3 · Notifications as a mixer

Account › Notifications. One fader per topic; up is louder: in app only, morning summary, email, push.
Now · switches
In-app always; email and push per person
Strips · mixer, 4 levels (rows on a phone)

Might be betterThe whole mix is visible at once, and “what” is split from “how loud”, which the switches blur.Might notEmail versus push is not louder for everyone, and four faders take more room than four rows on a phone.

4 · If it goes everywhere

Every ordered choice with 3 or more stops in the prototype, as the same stepped strip. Switches (yes/no) and unordered choices (accounts, formats, Dark/Light) stay as they are.

What it showsThe same strip as above in all 12 places: settings rows, onboarding, a dialog, phones, 3–5 stops.What it costsA new design-system component (Figma first, both widths) replacing the Select in 10 rows and the segmented control in 2, and every screen rebuilt.

Tasks for the session

Read aloud; the same for both versions. Half start with Now, half with the strips. Run tasks 1–6 in one skin (Aluminium · TX or Pocket · TE, alternating per person), then switch skins for task 7.
  1. You want half the fee up front. Set the deposit for new offers.
  2. Your venues pay late a lot. Make reminders firmer.
  3. You only want your phone to buzz for gig-day changes; money news can wait for the morning summary.
  4. Laptop, keyboard only: repeat task 1.
  5. From sign-up, go through the intro to Your band and set how many you are.
  6. Look at section 4. Imagine every one of these settings looked like this: good or tiring?
  7. Switch the Skin (Aluminium · TX ↔ Pocket · TE) and repeat task 3. Which one would you use every day, and why?
  8. Which would you rather have in LAAS, and why? What did the strips make you think of?
