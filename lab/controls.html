Buttons, pads and switches

After Akai (MPC, MPK mini) and Teenage Engineering (EP-133, TX-6, OP-1 field, TP-7). Each one sits next to the LAAS control it would replace. Back to the lab

Demo only. A sheet to judge the language. Like the strip, any of these would have to replace every control of its kind, and only makes sense as part of a whole hardware look.

Two-label key

EP-133: main function on top, shift function below; one orange key for the irreversible one

In LAAS: secondary and primary buttons; the second label is the "more" action (today a ⋯ menu). Doubt: a hidden shift layer is expert-only.

Round inlay button

TX-6 FX buttons: a ring with a coloured inlay, label printed under

In LAAS: the step and dialog actions (Back, Continue, Send). Doubt: 72 px round buttons take more room than the 44 px pill.

Backlit pads

MPC / MPK mini pads: rubber, lit from underneath; the colour says the state
amber = needs you (an unread or a blocker), accent = chosen

In LAAS: big choice tiles (Pick your look, Add to chat), and the "needs you" count chips. Doubt: colour alone can't carry state; keep the text.

Backlit function buttons

MPC function buttons: unlit = not available, dim = available, lit = on

In LAAS: filter buttons and chips (Bookings, Money filters). The three LED states map to off / available / on. Doubt: amber and green also mean attention and paid in LAAS.

Lever toggle

The bat switch on the EQ and channel-strip references: ON up, OFF down
Late interest: on

In LAAS: the Toggle (142:635, .acc-tg), 14 switches. Doubt: 96 px tall; a settings row is 44.

Slide switch

Original OP-1 power slider, hardware mode switches: 2–3 fixed positions

In LAAS: the segmented control (Appearance, Dashboard starts with, Single/EP). Fits: same height as today, same keyboard (arrows).
