Ask bar lab

The real bar, CSS and fold script (views/abarfold.js), alone. Wide screens only: the stage must be 600 px or wider for the idle fold.

Timings

A · Fold when idle

Then: hover the pill (it lightens and the sparkle turns, it stays folded), click it or Tab to it to open; move the pointer away with the field empty and it folds back after about 1.6 s.

B · Sparkle wink

Automatic: folded for the first time span, then at most once per the second, three times per visit, never while scrolling.

C · Manual hide (the X, ⌘J)

The X and ⌘J fold the bar into its pill with the same spring as the idle fold; a click on the pill or ⌘J opens it. (The mini Ask pill is now only the narrow-screen fallback.)
